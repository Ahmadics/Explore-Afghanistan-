DISCOVER KABUL

The Heart of Afghanistan

Kabul is the capital and largest city of Afghanistan. Located in a valley surrounded by mountains, the city has been an important center of political, cultural, and commercial life for centuries.

Its streets and neighborhoods reflect different periods of Afghanistan's history, from historic landmarks and gardens to modern urban areas and busy markets.

HISTORY & HERITAGE

A City with a Long History

Kabul has played an important role in the history of the region for many centuries.

The city has been influenced by different civilizations, kingdoms, and empires. Its location connected Central Asia, South Asia, and the wider region through historic routes.

Today, Kabul contains historic buildings, mosques, gardens, markets, and neighborhoods that reflect different chapters of the city's development.

PLACES TO DISCOVER

Landmarks, Gardens & Culture

Kabul offers a variety of historic and cultural places to explore. Among them are historic gardens, religious landmarks, traditional markets, and important public spaces.

Bagh-e Babur, one of the city's best-known historic gardens, is associated with Babur, the founder of the Mughal Empire. The garden is also included on Afghanistan's UNESCO Tentative List.
