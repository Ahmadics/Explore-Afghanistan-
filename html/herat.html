DISCOVER HERAT

The Cultural Heart of Western Afghanistan

Herat is one of Afghanistan's most historic cities. Located in the western part of the country, it has been an important center of culture, trade, education, and art for centuries.

The city is known for its historic monuments, traditional architecture, gardens, markets, and rich cultural heritage. Herat's location also connected Afghanistan with neighboring regions through historic trade routes.

HISTORY & HERITAGE

A City of History & Art

Herat has been an important cultural and historical center for many centuries.

The city has been influenced by different civilizations, dynasties, and empires throughout its long history. Its location made Herat an important connection between Central Asia, Persia, and South Asia.

Today, Herat remains known for its historic architecture, traditional crafts, religious monuments, gardens, and cultural traditions that reflect the city's long and diverse past.

PLACES TO DISCOVER

Architecture, Heritage & Culture

Herat offers many historic and cultural places to explore. The city is especially recognized for its traditional architecture, historic mosques, gardens, markets, and cultural landmarks.

The Herat Citadel, the Friday Mosque, and the Musalla complex are among the city's best-known historic sites and reflect the importance of Herat as a center of architecture and culture.
